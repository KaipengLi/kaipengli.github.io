🚲 Our collaborative project, “Optimization of Port Placement and Bicycle Management for Tsukuchari,” has been published in the University of Tsukuba Data Science Case Bank. We explored data-driven methods for improving the revenue structure of Tsukuba City’s bike-sharing service through demand prediction and vehicle redistribution optimization.
