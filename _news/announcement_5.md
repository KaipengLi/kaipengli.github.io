🎉 I am honored to receive the **Outstanding Graduate Certificate** from the University of Tsukuba upon completing my Master's degree.

This recognition is a meaningful milestone in my academic journey. During my Master's study, I worked on information retrieval, recommender systems, and generative retrieval, with a particular focus on building effective and scalable retrieval systems.

I would like to sincerely thank my supervisor, [Prof. Haitao Yu](https://ii-research-yu.github.io/), for his continuous guidance and support. I am also grateful to my collaborators, lab members, friends, and the [University of Tsukuba](https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/) community for their encouragement throughout this journey.
